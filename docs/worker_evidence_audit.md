# Worker evidence and denominator audit

I inspected the original worker sources, transcribed the selected published cells and recorded their denominator limitations.

Government Census workbooks, Coal Ministry PDFs and PLFS documentation are bundled with source hashes. Published local-study facts are transcribed with source page and original denominator, and their full producer publications are linked. No raw individual data were obtained from either study.

- Census: exact hierarchy filters and sex/residence/group/state arithmetic checks; NIC 05-09 includes support services. Employment-status duration is distinct from contractual formality.
- Coal: full workforce table distinguished from disability and reservation-quantum tables. 2024 count = 231,058; 2026 = 214,333. No matched hiring/exit decomposition acquired.
- SECL: advertised categories sum to 350 and filled categories to 345; UR not caste, advertised not applicants.
- Arisa/CLRA: Budhpura 47 individual interviews includes 10 different-instrument home-workers. Main-questionnaire base 37 retained for piece rate/PPE. Makrana N=50; no caste×route cross-tab. Composition paragraph and advance discrepancy are preserved.
- ILO: worker sample 115, owner sample 43. Rounded groups 34/30/30/5 sum to 99; no invented 115-row individual dataset. PF subgroup denominators not inferred from combined N.
- PLFS: documentation only. No wage or hiring model claimed; registration and survey-design details required for unit records.

Controlled duration models are descriptive and exploratory. The 35-cluster covariance is a regional model, not enumeration/design error. Many absorbed W4 strata and omitted age/education/occupation controls limit inference. Future caste/job-entry and firm-growth causal work requires a real matched panel and an identification design.
