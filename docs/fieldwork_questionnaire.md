# Proposed fieldwork instrument

I drafted this instrument for future piloting, translation and data collection. I have not collected responses to these questions.

The CSV supplies 86 draft measures across consent, business, owner, contacts, financial outcomes, institutions, follow-up and qualitative modules. These are questions to pilot and translate, not responses or a validated instrument. The report elaborates sampling, relationships, ethics and outcomes.

Interview the knowledgeable owner or manager in a safe setting. Obtain separate consent for optional identity, contact and administrative-linkage modules. Use respondent self-description only; never infer caste from surnames, locality or an alter's name. Unknown and declined classifications remain separate. Record the function and timing of assistance, unsuccessful requests and relevant opportunity sets. Repeat outcome definitions at 12 and 24 months. Store identities and contact rosters outside the public repository.
